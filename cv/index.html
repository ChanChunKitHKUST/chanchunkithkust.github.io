Profile

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at HKUST, supported by the Hong Kong PhD Fellowship and advised by Prof. Yangqiu Song. My research brings together Large Language Models, Theory of Mind, Mental World Models, and Post-Training.

I investigate how models infer and revise beliefs, desires, and intentions, and how these representations can support reasoning and action. My goal is to connect mental-state understanding with better decisions through Mental World Models, reinforcement fine-tuning, and agents that adapt to people and changing contexts.

Research Interests

Theory of Mind & Mental World Models

I study how language models infer beliefs, desires, and intentions. My goal is to develop Mental World Models that track these states over time, revise them with new evidence, and support socially informed reasoning.

Post-Training for Decision-Making

I investigate how mental-state understanding can improve decisions through Post-Training. My work combines belief–desire–intention supervision and reinforcement fine-tuning to predict customer behavior in e-commerce.

Agents

I study how agents infer others’ intentions and use that understanding to make decisions. My work examines Theory of Mind in negotiation and cooperative games, alongside my interest in mental-state-guided planning and tool use.
